What We Actually Do 🔧

Real work. Done by real humans. Who genuinely enjoy this stuff (we've checked).

🩺 Instance Health Monitoring

We check that your database is alive and not silently planning its own demise. Alerts, dashboards, proactive fixes before things explode.

👤 User & Access Management

Adding users, removing users, making sure Dave from accounting didn't accidentally get DBA privileges again. Classic Dave.

📦 Space & Tablespace Management

Monitoring disk usage, adding datafiles, extending tablespaces before they fill up at 11pm on a Friday. Very fun to prevent.

🔄 Patch Management

Applying patches, PSUs, security fixes. Not the most glamorous work, but critical unless you enjoy news articles about your company.

📋 Log & Alert Monitoring

Reading alert logs so you don't have to. We actually enjoy finding ORA-600 errors at dawn. (One of us does, anyway.)

⚙️ Startup / Shutdown Procedures

Controlled maintenance windows, orderly shutdowns, graceful restarts. No "have you tried turning it off and on again" energy here.

🐢➡️🚀 Query Tuning

That query running for 4 hours? Probably runs in 3 seconds after we look at it. Execution plans are our love language.

📊 AWR / Statspack Analysis

Deep dive into performance reports, identifying bottlenecks, wait events, and the query someone wrote in 2009 that's still haunting your system.

🗂️ Index Optimization

Creating, rebuilding, dropping indexes. Too few? Slow queries. Too many? Slow inserts. We find the sweet spot.

🧹 Schema & Statistics Maintenance

Updating optimizer statistics, analyzing tables, making sure the query planner isn't making hilariously wrong decisions.

🔧 Memory & Parameter Tuning

SGA, PGA, buffer pools — tweaking the knobs that make your database hum rather than wheeze.

🔎 Execution Plan Stabilization

SQL Plan Baselines, hints, outlines — making sure Oracle doesn't randomly decide to full-scan a 2TB table because Mercury is in retrograde.

💾 RMAN Backup Management

Configuring, scheduling, monitoring backups. Testing them too — because an untested backup is just a promise.

🔁 Data Guard Configuration

Setting up standby databases, managing switchovers and failovers. Your Plan B, professionally maintained.

🏥 Point-in-Time Recovery

"Someone deleted the production table." Don't panic. We've brought databases back from worse. Much worse.

📅 Retention Policy Management

How long should backups live? We'll configure policies that satisfy compliance, storage, and your sanity simultaneously.

🧪 Restore Testing

Regular test restores to verify your backups actually work. Revolutionary concept, we know.

📡 Replication Monitoring

Keeping an eye on lag, errors, and sync status so your replicas stay, well, replicas and not divergent alternate timelines.

🔍 Full Database Audits

Deep inspection of your setup — security, performance, configuration, best practices. We write a report. You implement it. Everyone wins.

🚀 Database Migrations

Moving to a new server, upgrading versions, migrating to cloud. We've done it. We'll do it calmly and without drama.

🏗️ New Database Builds

Setting up a new Oracle/Postgres/MSSQL instance from scratch, properly, with all the right configs from day one.

🔐 Security Hardening

Locking down access, auditing privileges, removing default users, making your database harder to hack than it currently is (no offense).

📚 Documentation

Writing the runbooks and diagrams your future self will be grateful for at 2am during an incident. You're welcome in advance.

🎓 Consulting & Code Review

Developer wrote something unspeakable in SQL? We'll review it diplomatically. Or bluntly. Client's choice.
